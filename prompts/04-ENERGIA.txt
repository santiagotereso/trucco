# ENERGÍA — El instante antes de explotar  [deporte]

Create an intense high-end storyboard contact sheet for a 30-second sports campaign, presented as 12 cinematic widescreen frames in a 4x3 grid. Follow one elite female sprinter through the seconds before and during a race: empty stadium before sunrise, athlete entering the tunnel, close-up of spikes touching concrete, hands tightening laces, muscles activating during warm-up, breath visible in cold air, fingers settling onto the starting line, extreme close-up of focused eyes, explosive start from the blocks, lateral tracking shot at maximum speed, abstract motion blur and flying track particles, final portrait after crossing the finish line-exhausted, powerful and calm. Visual language focused on compressed energy becoming movement. Deep blacks, metallic blues, sharp sunrise highlights, sweat, skin texture, low camera angles, macro details, high-speed photography, anamorphic flares, dynamic motion blur, premium international sportswear commercial. Maintain the same athlete, outfit and stadium across every shot. No text, logos, crowds or duplicated compositions.
