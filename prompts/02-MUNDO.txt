# MUNDO — Un universo dentro del plato  [restaurantes]

Create a surreal high-end storyboard contact sheet for a 30-second fine-dining commercial, composed of 12 cinematic widescreen frames in a precise 4x3 grid. The central idea is "an entire world hidden inside a dish." Begin with an extreme macro of a drop of sauce, then travel inside it as if entering another universe: salt crystals become mountains, olive oil becomes a golden ocean, herbs move like a forest in the wind, steam becomes clouds, a red pepper landscape glows at sunset, liquid pours like a waterfall, a chef walks through this imagined culinary world, ingredients orbit in elegant slow motion, the fantasy collapses back into the plate, final garnishing touches, a diner discovers the dish, and an iconic overhead hero shot closes the film. Sophisticated magical realism, tactile ingredients, rich natural colors, photorealistic food, macro probe-lens cinematography, seamless transitions, elegant and mysterious rather than whimsical. Consistent plate, ingredients and art direction. No typography, no logos, no childish fantasy.
